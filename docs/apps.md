# Hosted apps

Each app is an Ansible role (`ansible/roles/<role>`) deployed by the playbook of its host, and its Pangolin route is an entry in `tofu/pangolin_config/website_<name>.tf`. Unless noted, Pangolin asks for SSO login before it lets a request through.

| [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org/) | `jellyfin` | NAS | Media server (films, series, music, books, audiobooks from the `media` shared folder, read-only), Quick Sync transcoding. Not public, one name everywhere: **`https://media.sylvain.cloud`**, with or without `:8443`, at home and outside with the Pangolin client (role `jellyfin`; the private resource of that alias, TCP 443 and 8443, served by the NAS's own Newt, site `nas`). Without a port at home only while UGOS's HTTPS redirect setting stays unticked; `:8443` never depends on it. The public A record points at the NAS's LAN IP (`tofu/dns/media.tf`); the NAS's Traefik (role `nas_proxy`, `dc@nas_proxy`, container `nas-proxy`) terminates TLS with a Let's Encrypt certificate (DNS-01, OVH key `nas_ovh_*`), listening on 443 and 8443; 8443 is published by Docker, the host's 443 is taken by a relay container in host networking (`nas-proxy-443`, PROXY protocol), so a 443 grabbed back by UGOS's nginx only breaks the URL without a port. Fallback: `http://192.168.1.137:8096` on the LAN. Jellyfin's "Known proxies" must hold Traefik's fixed IP on Jellyfin's `media` network, `172.30.96.10`. Jellyfin accounts of its own, no SSO. User guide (apps, casting, file naming, transcoding, ffmpeg): [media.md](media.md). Its config and database (`/volume1/docker/jellyfin/config`, SQLite copied by `sqlite3`, `VACUUM INTO` in WAL) are backed up nightly by borgmatic in a container on the NAS (monitor « Backup Jellyfin », `tofu/pangolin_config/backup_jellyfin.tf`; restore-tested); the media are not in borg, only protected by the RAID 1 and the Btrfs snapshots |

PostgreSQL 17. Private only: `http://mediamanager.internal:8000` through the Pangolin client (role `mediamanager`, site `nas`); the qBittorrent and Prowlarr UIs on the NAS's `127.0.0.1` only (SSH tunnel). Config backed up by borgmatic, **not its database** (no `docker exec` from the NAS's borgmatic container). Not in borg (opt-in). Setup and recovery: [runbooks/kdrive-nas.md](runbooks/kdrive-nas.md) |

The NAS admin page and Proxmox are not public: they are Pangolin private resources (`nas.sylvain.cloud`, `pve.internal`, `tofu/pangolin_config/private_resources.tf`), served by the Newt LXC. The NAS admin page (UGOS) is **`https://nas.sylvain.cloud`** (or `:8443`) at home and through the Pangolin client, the same NAS Traefik in front of UGOS's nginx (public A record to the NAS's LAN IP, `tofu/dns/nas.tf`); fallback `http://192.168.1.137:9999`.

This table is the reference list of the apps; AGENTS.md points here instead of repeating it. The playbooks are `ansible/docker.yml` (Docker VM), `ansible/pi.yml` (Raspberry Pi), `ansible/flip.yml` (flip), `ansible/pangolin.yaml` (Pangolin VM), `ansible/lxc.yml` (the Newt LXC: Blocky) and `ansible/nas.yml` (the NAS, run by hand: NFS exports, the Beszel agent, its Newt, Jellyfin, the NAS's Traefik, `nas_proxy`, and the kDrive copy, `kdrive_sync`). To add, pause or remove an app, see the `new-app` and `remove-app` skills under `.claude/skills/` and [conventions/app-lifecycle.md](conventions/app-lifecycle.md).
