# tofu/dns

Moved from `AGENTS.md` (agent-facing notes, kept in English); the index there links here.

`tofu/dns/` — OVH DNS zone records (`ovh_domain_zone_record` / `ovh_domain_zone_redirection`, not Cloudflare): the Pangolin A records (`*.sylvain.cloud`, the `sylvain.cloud` apex, `*.sylvain.dev`, and the `sylvain.dev` apex while `sylvain_dev_apex_to_pangolin` is true) read from `tofu/pangolin`'s state, the A records `media.sylvain.cloud` and `nas.sylvain.cloud` to the NAS's LAN IP (`media.tf`, `nas.tf`: they beat the wildcard and serve the NAS's own Traefik, at home and through the Pangolin client), the GitHub Pages challenges, the betisier redirect, and the `sylvain.dev` apex Google Search Console TXT record (`search_console.tf`), and the mail authentication of the four OVH zones (`mail.tf`: SPF, DMARC with aggregate reports to `dmarc_report_address` plus the `<zone>._report._dmarc` authorizations, TLS-RPT; sylvain.cloud, which sends and receives nothing, locked down with `v=spf1 -all` and `p=reject` (null MX behind `sylvain_cloud_null_mx`, off until OVH is shown to accept `0 .`); DKIM keys stay with Infomaniak/SimpleLogin; procedure and hardening ladder: `docs/runbooks/dmarc.md`). CAA at each zone apex (`caa.tf`): `letsencrypt.org` only, plus `pki.goog` and `ssl.com` on sylvain.dev, whose Cloudflare Pages names (CNAMEs to `*.pages.dev`, which publishes no CAA, so the lookup climbs to the apex) get certificates from whichever of the three Cloudflare picks; `issuewild ";"` everywhere (no wildcard is issued), `iodef` to `dmarc_report_address`. A new CA or a wildcard certificate needs its entry there first, or issuance fails (`docs/runbooks/dmarc.md`, « CAA »). Records that predate a resource are adopted with the `tofu import` CLI, never an `import` block. Cloudflare only appears in the Pages modules (`tofu/ref`, `tofu/site`, `tofu/1y`, `tofu/planning`).
